# Routin — Research and Systems Design

**Status:** research and architecture, not an implemented product. **Evidence cutoff:** 2026-11-16.

Routin is an adaptive AI personal secretary for coordinating professional and personal responsibilities, protecting time and attention, learning explicit and inferred preferences responsibly, and verifying delegated outcomes.

This repository is a source-traceable research and design workspace. Each paper has an individual critical review and, where licensing permits, a local source artifact. Source acquisition status is explicit; a bibliographic link is never represented as a downloaded paper.

## Start here

- `docs/` — product vision, ontology, system architecture, modules, workflows and algorithms.
- `literature-reviews/` — structured individual paper reviews and cross-paper syntheses.
- `papers/` — categorized paper sources, acquisition manifests, licenses and fetch tooling.
- `decisions/` — architecture decision records with evidence and trade-offs.
- `research/` — hypotheses, experiments, evaluations, evidence ledger and research questions.
- `schemas/` — machine-readable domain and event contracts.

**Policy:** no UI implementation until the decision policy, information hierarchy, and backend behavioral requirements are specified and validated. No assertion of scientific novelty without a prior-art review and comparative evidence.
