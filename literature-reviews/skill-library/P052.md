## 1. Research problem and actual methodology
Voyager combines automatic curriculum, accumulating reusable executable skills and iterative feedback-driven skill refinement in a Minecraft environment. Long-term capabilities compound through skill retrieval and composition.

## 2. Findings supported by the assessed source
Minecraft skill execution is repeatable and observable in a sandbox. Real emails and interpersonal agreements can be irreversible and socially consequential; trajectory success in Minecraft does not justify auto-execution in personal life.

The findings apply to the paper's studied population, benchmark and method. A derived design for Routin is an engineering hypothesis, not an observed effect of the paper.

## 3. Assumptions, caveats and counterexamples
Routin should maintain an explicitly versioned, permission-scoped procedure catalog with precondition checks, postcondition verification and quarantine after failures. Skills are never mere memories or strings executable directly from external content.
