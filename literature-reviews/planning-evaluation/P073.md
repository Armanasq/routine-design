# P073: Agent Planning Benchmark: A Diagnostic Framework for Planning Capabilities in LLM Agents

**Authors:** Haoyu Sun, Wenxuan Wang, Mingyang Song, Jujie He, Weinan Zhang, Yang Liu, Yang Yang, Yu Cheng  
**Year/venue:** 2026; Findings of EMNLP 2026  
**Canonical source:** https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1041/  
**Access level:** acl_anthology_abstract_and_arxiv_metadata. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Separates planning from execution in a diagnostic benchmark with 4,209 multimodal cases across 22 domains and five settings. Includes holistic and feedback-conditioned planning, irrelevant tools, broken tools and unsolvable conditions, and downstream validation on ToolSandbox and tau^2-bench subsets.

## What the evidence supports
Reported models remain weak in long-horizon plan correctness, robustness to tool noise and refusal/unsolvable task diagnosis. Planning-guided refinement improved downstream metrics for representative models in the paper's evaluation.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Benchmarks measure constructed task environments rather than actual ongoing user commitments, and planning quality does not itself authorize an external effect. Coverage depends on 2026 tested models and modalities.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Create separate metrics and proof obligations for goal interpretation, tool discovery, feasibility, plan correction, execution and postcondition verification. Distinguish planner search timeout from actual missing capability.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Evaluate held-out domains with broken/unavailable tools, contradictory evidence, search budget exhaustion and currently unsolvable tasks. Require useful partial assistance rather than unconditional refusal.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
