# P061 — Just-in-Time Adaptive Interventions (JITAIs) in Mobile Health: Key Components and Design Principles for Ongoing Health Behavior Support

**Authors:** Inbal Nahum-Shani, Shawna N. Smith, Bonnie J. Spring, Linda M. Collins et al.  
**Publication:** 2018, Annals of Behavioral Medicine 52(6), 446–462  
**Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s12160-016-9830-8  
**Review evidence:** `open_access_full_text_framework_sections_and_example_rules`, verified on 2026-11-16. This is an original critical synthesis; methods/results are inspected only to the degree indicated by the access label.

## 1. Question and investigated methods
Foundational intervention-design paper articulating distal/proximal outcomes, decision points, tailoring variables, intervention options and decision rules, together with contextual vulnerability/opportunity and receptivity. It supplies a design process and examples.

## 2. Empirical result or theoretical contribution
Effective design requires deciding whether intervention is useful *now*, what form/dose it should take, and when intervention is unwelcome or burdensome. Includes a no-intervention option when criteria are not met.

## 3. Boundaries, assumptions, negative evidence
The framework alone does not establish optimal numeric thresholds, nor does it prove every JITAI improves long-term health. Context sensing and receptivity estimation are uncertain and potentially privacy-invasive.

## 4. Concrete Routin policy or architectural implication
Routin's attention/intervention policy should have explicit NOOP, proximal vs distal outcome distinction, user receptivity, interruption burden and evidence gating. Apply beyond health only as a tested analogy.

## 5. Cross-disciplinary mechanism synthesis
Couple attention control with cross-domain capacity and goals: a well-timed nudge can still displace a critical obligation. Pair with self-regulation P065, COM-B P062 and learning evidence P063.

## 6. Falsifiable test and acceptance
Compare fixed reminders, importance-ranked prompts and context-adaptive rules including NOOP; stratify urgent safety needs, learning practice, unwanted intrusion, dropout and actual distal objective progress.
