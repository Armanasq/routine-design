## 1. Research problem and what was actually studied
Two-week deployment with 12 participants and 336 conversations found useful planning/reflection but rigidity, premature turn-taking, and overpromising.

## 2. Established finding / intellectual contribution
Short small-sample conversational deployment; not evidence of long-term goal or productivity improvement.

This statement is attributed to the cited research in its studied setting. It is **not** proof that the proposed Routin feature improves productivity.

## 3. Assumptions, limitations and alternative explanations
Voice agent must wait for full user intent, support interruption and correction, and avoid falsely claiming to have acted.
