# P082: Agents' Last Exam

**Authors:** Yiyou Sun, Xinyang Han, Weichen Zhang and extensive coauthors  
**Year/venue:** 2026; arXiv:2606.05405 preprint  
**Canonical source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05405  
**Access level:** arxiv_abstract_and_project_overview. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Constructs sustained expert-sourced long-horizon professional tasks across an extensible occupational taxonomy and assesses outcomes rather than isolated prompt correctness. Tasks represent varied non-physical industries, with code-verifiable evaluation where practical.

## What the evidence supports
Reports large remaining gap on hardest meaningful real work, reinforcing that current benchmark success does not imply universal end-to-end competence. Varying paper versions report different task counts; treat counts as versioned.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Expert-derived professional tasks are not necessarily representative of private user life, clinical care or interpersonal outcomes. End-to-end scoring can obscure why planning, missing tools, authority or execution failed.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Evaluate complete responsibility episodes, including externally verified outcomes, user effort and task complexity, and inspect attribution of failure to planning/discovery/execution versus unsupported competence.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Construct genuinely new task combinations in held-out industries and user contexts. Report success, complete trace, intervention overhead and unsafe effects, avoiding success claims from generated reports alone.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
