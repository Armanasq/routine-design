## 1. Research problem and what was actually studied
Interview-based study of administrative assistants, principals and managers examines monitoring, preparing, coordinating and filtering work.

## 2. Established finding / intellectual contribution
Not an experiment measuring the productivity of AI assistance; organizational practices may not carry over unchanged to personal life.

This statement is attributed to the cited research in its studied setting. It is **not** proof that the proposed Routin feature improves productivity.
