## 1. Research problem and what was actually studied
Proposes saga-inspired persistent context, planning validation and compensation for complex multi-step LLM workflows; reports benchmark consistency gains.

## 2. Established finding / intellectual contribution
Benchmarks do not demonstrate that multi-agent orchestration is needed for basic secretary operations; implementation complexity is significant.

This statement is attributed to the cited research in its studied setting. It is **not** proof that the proposed Routin feature improves productivity.

## 3. Assumptions, limitations and alternative explanations
Separate typed plan validation and durable execution. Prefer one coordinator with deterministic state over unbounded multi-agent debates.
