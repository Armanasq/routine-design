## Why more data is insufficient
Frické critiques the DIKW pyramid, especially the suggestion that processing data automatically yields information, knowledge and wisdom. The work is argumentation in information science. It does not test Routin, but it directly challenges a naïve research pipeline of collecting papers and asserting expertise.

## Practical replacement
Classify artifacts by epistemic and normative type: observation, ambiguous claim, defeasible belief, user value, intention, external commitment, authorization, uncertain forecast, decision and verified outcome. Every transition requires evidence appropriate for the type. A high-confidence language model prediction does not become an observed fact or a permission grant.

## Contradictory example
Three connected calendars show 20:00 meetings. The user's explicit instruction forbids evening meetings unless approved. The appropriate conclusion is that past observations conflict with a current value or constraint, not that the user prefers evening work.

## Interconnections
AGM belief revision (P044) informs contraction and supersession; Bratman (P039) distinguishes practical intention; Clark–Brennan (P037) separates social agreement from one person's assertion.

## Test
Adversarially combine stale, ambiguous and conditional evidence. Measure false type promotions and downstream action error against summarization-and-act models. Exposing a useful limitation is preferable to fabricating an answer.
