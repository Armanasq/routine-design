# P071: Plan or Retrieve First? A Scale Study of Tool Discovery Architectures

**Authors:** Mingfei Zhang, Amit Surana, Ziauddin Majid, Chenxi Wang, Michael Xu  
**Year/venue:** 2026; EMNLP 2026 Industry Track  
**Canonical source:** https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-industry.19/  
**Access level:** conference_abstract_and_arxiv_introduction_inspected. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Controlled scale study contrasts Retrieve-then-Plan (query->API retrieval->planning) and Plan-then-Retrieve (generate abstract tool stubs->retrieve->plan) across ToolBench subsets of 46,984 APIs and one-tool, intra-category, cross-category tasks. Varies retrieval budgets and registry sizes and compares two model families.

## What the evidence supports
One-step lookup favors RtP; multi-tool tasks favor PtR in the reported tests. PtR's abstract stubs can be nonexistent functions; additional discovery inference approximately doubles per-query inference cost in the paper's setup. Strong causal claim applies to this specific architecture and evaluation setting.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Task classification may be uncertain before observation; stub-to-real-API mapping can hallucinate; all results depend on ToolBench construction, model and retrieval indexing. No direct evidence of personal user outcome, external authorization or long-horizon execution safety.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Use complexity-adaptive choice between direct search and abstract effect decomposition. Always ground candidate stubs to available trusted operators and never treat a stub name as executable. Keep discovery query cost in the controller.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Under identical registry/tool budgets, compare RtP, PtR, hybrid and all-tools prompt on withheld multi-domain tasks. Report all-required-tool recall, wrong tool binding, false API names, latency, cost, and real verified outcome.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
