# P051 — ExpeL: LLM Agents Are Experiential Learners

## 1. Research problem and actual methodology
An agent collects training-task experiences, extracts natural-language insights and reuses them with retrieved examples at inference. It studies transfer of learned experiences across tasks without parametric model updates.

## 2. Findings supported by the assessed source
Transfer and performance claims come from evaluated agent environments rather than real-world communication permissions, external outcomes or human values. Insight extraction may overgeneralize across superficially similar tasks.

The findings apply to the paper's studied population, benchmark and method. A derived design for Routin is an engineering hypothesis, not an observed effect of the paper.

## 3. Assumptions, caveats and counterexamples
Routin should promote repeated verified episodes to candidate procedures with applicability predicates, negative examples and an expiry. New user/account/recipient contexts must be evaluated rather than assumed equivalent.

## 4. Consequence for Routin's cognitive architecture
Builds on Reflexion P050; combined with MAGE P046, extracted insight should reference a valid execution branch; with Voyager P052, converted skills need tests, versioning and permission gates.

## 5. Cross-paper mechanism and what it enables
Test new tasks with changed recipient, API, or authorization and report negative transfer, false actions and verified completion, not just answer similarity.
