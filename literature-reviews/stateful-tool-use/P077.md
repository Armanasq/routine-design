# P077: tau-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains

**Authors:** Shunyu Yao, Noah Shinn, Pedram Razavi, Karthik Narasimhan  
**Year/venue:** 2024; arXiv:2406.12045 (preprint)  
**Canonical source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045  
**Access level:** arxiv_abstract_and_task_example. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Simulates dynamic user conversations and enterprise-style domain API tools/policies. Evaluates final database state against specified goal states and reports repeated-run pass^k to reveal reliability variance.

## What the evidence supports
Even strong 2024 function-calling systems performed below 50% per-task in the evaluated tasks; repeated pass^8 for retail is reported below 25%. The study underlines reliability and policy-following rather than one plausible transcript.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Results reflect 2024 agents, benchmark tasks, and simulated humans; do not reuse performance as 2026 production capability estimates. Database state also may not verify human agreement or physical outcomes.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Benchmark real state transitions and repeated consistency. Domain-specific policy constraints must be checked outside generated response text; a single successful demo is insufficient.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Measure actual external state across repeated stochastic runs, and require no unauthorized effects. Add missed confirmations, partial acknowledgments and user corrections.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
