## 1. Research problem and actual methodology
Proposes complementary learning systems: fast, pattern-separated hippocampal encoding of individual episodes and slower interleaved neocortical learning of statistical structure, motivated by interference between rapid learning and prior generalization.

## 2. Findings supported by the assessed source
This is a cognitive-neuroscience/computational theory, not evidence that AI agents should run a human-like hippocampus or that a given number of days is necessary for consolidation.

The findings apply to the paper's studied population, benchmark and method. A derived design for Routin is an engineering hypothesis, not an observed effect of the paper.

## 3. Assumptions, caveats and counterexamples
Use the distinction to separate immediately recorded experience from slowly promoted personal preference/procedure generalizations. Keep contextual exceptions episodic; accumulate multiple independent observations before proposing general rules; distinguish deletion from recency forgetting.
