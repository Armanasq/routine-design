# P079: Agent Lightning: Train ANY AI Agents with Reinforcement Learning

**Authors:** Xufang Luo, Yuge Zhang, Zhiyuan He, Zilong Wang, Siyun Zhao, Dongsheng Li, Luna K. Qiu, Yuqing Yang  
**Year/venue:** 2025; arXiv:2508.03680 preprint  
**Canonical source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03680  
**Access level:** arxiv_abstract_and_introduction. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Separates deploy-time agent execution from training trajectories through a unified transition interface and hierarchical credit assignment, allowing training of arbitrary agent harnesses including multi-agent/dynamic tool flows.

## What the evidence supports
Authors report stable performance gains on evaluated text-to-SQL, retrieval and math tool-use tasks. Contribution is a framework and training method; it does not prove online learning is safe from users' private history.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Training may optimize inadequate proxy rewards; full outcome credit assignment in real-world administrative work is often unobserved, delayed or shared with other people. Consent, forgetting and negative transfer require independent checks.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Keep stateful runtime/harness and learning pipeline decoupled. Train only from consented, audited, appropriately attributed traces; retain versioned rollback and separate hard permission checks.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Offline first: compare rule-based and memory-conditioned adaptation with optional trained policy on held-out domains; report reward hacking, degradation of protected goals and unauthorized tool attempts.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
