# P080: ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models

**Authors:** Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, Nan Du, Izhak Shafran, Karthik Narasimhan, Yuan Cao  
**Year/venue:** 2023; ICLR 2023  
**Canonical source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629  
**Access level:** arxiv_official_abstract_and_conference_metadata. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Interleaves language-model reasoning and external actions to update plans and handle exceptions in tasks like question answering, ALFWorld and WebShop, as opposed to reasoning without environmental actions.

## What the evidence supports
Demonstrates value of observe/act feedback compared with tested prompting/RL baselines in those environments; establishes broad prior art for interactive agent loops.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Natural-language reasoning traces are not a trustworthy source of permissions, provider effects or source evidence. ReAct alone does not solve temporal memory validity, durable retries or long-term learning.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Adopt observe/reason/act only inside typed decision-and-execution contracts; run deterministic domain/permission checks before effects; record actual observations and verify outcomes.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Compare a ReAct-style prompt agent, scripted task router and typed capability planner on identical held-out domain cases, measuring hallucinated tools, follow-up correctness, unauthorized effects and cost.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
