# S19 — Portable Coordination, Nonportable Expertise

**Sources:** P059 Nature personal health agent, P060 longitudinal health, P069 configurable JITAI templates, P058 memory-induced tool drift, P052 reusable skills, P027 saga effects, WHO and FDA technical guidance.

## Why this matters

A reusable procedure that works for one work email may be technically executable in a medical context but ethically or legally invalid. One user granted permission to move an ordinary meeting; the same grant must not allow disclosing health notes to a clinician, uploading an employment document, or making a financial payment.

Universality should therefore transfer **only the safe shared skeleton** (observe, parse, plan, authorize, execute, verify) and require domain competence, consent, evidence and outcome conditions to be re-established for each type of effect.

## Compatibility vector

Procedure transfer is valid only if (entity semantics, intended end, data scope, evidence reliability, identity, authority type, irreversibility, validation, verification) are compatible. Any unknown dimension yields simulation/shadow evaluation or abstention. Semantic similarity of previous instructions has no authority.

The 2019 JITAI template/rule framework already demonstrates configurable domain interventions, so claiming novelty for a generic pack DSL is not justified. Potential contribution is **compositional validation under heterogeneous risks, goals and changing permissions**.

## Example

Coordinate an appointment: scheduling a normal meeting and a medical appointment share calendar resources, but only the latter may involve sensitive notes and specialist safety rules. The work calendar pack can receive "busy at this time"; it cannot retrieve or transmit diagnosis or treatment details. A clinical reminder may be recorded as sent while the substantive medical outcome remains unknown.

## Tests

Cross-domain transfer matrix, wrong recipient, stale source, forbidden health advice, active instruction conflicting with a recalled habit, external provider timeout after possible effect, changed regulation, user withdraws sensitive access, and negative transfer of successful skill to unsupported domain.

## Acceptance

Zero unauthorized cross-domain effects/disclosures, no false domain outcome, bounded fallback when unsupported, reproducible policy and pack version trace. Merely adding multiple modules is not a research result.
