# S12 — Experience Is Not Yet a Skill

**Research:** Voyager P052 + ExpeL P051 + Reflexion P050 + Memory-R1 P055 + Memory-Induced Tool-Drift P058 + verified workflow prior art P027/P028.

## Connection

In simulated environments, agents can accumulate successful skill code or language lessons, but a personal secretary operates in a world with hard action rights, non-idempotent effects, social counterparts, private data and delayed confirmation. A single successful response cannot establish a safe reusable procedure.

A memory system may also change tool parameters inappropriately: P058 reports memory-induced tool drift from irrelevant personality-style memories. This makes personality/interaction memory **unsafe as an executable policy input without a typed enforcement layer**.

## Derived experience compiler

Record precise observation, intent, state, selected action, receipt, counterpart acknowledgment and verified outcome. Compare to failed or divergent episodes. Distill a procedure candidate with applicability predicate, negative applicability predicate, required identity, consent, explicit approval class, side-effect map, idempotency/recovery method and postcondition verifier. Put it through static validation, simulated failure injection and shadow comparison. Promote only after measured evidence; remember procedure version and expiration conditions.

The user's "prefer brief replies" may alter a drafted sentence but must not change recipient, payment amount, calendar semantics or authorization.

## Scientific issue

Outcome attribution is difficult: the trajectory succeeding does not mean every intermediate memory/strategy was causally helpful. The user may have supplied a missing piece or a counterparty may have agreed for independent reasons. Maintain honest unknown attribution unless experimental support exists.

## Experiment

Task variants with different recipients, partial permissions and uncertain provider effects; compare static script, prompted experiences, memory-conditioned proposed procedure, and certified typed procedure. Score successes, risky negative transfer, approval burden and cost per **verified** outcome.
