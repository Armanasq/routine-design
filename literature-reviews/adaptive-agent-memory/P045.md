## 1. Research problem and actual methodology
AdaMEM separates an offline long-term trajectory bank from dynamic short-term strategy memory generated at intermediate decisions. Unlike episode-init-only retrieval, the agent queries relevant experiences and synthesizes state-specific plans. A low-compute variant refreshes selectively; a high-compute variant refreshes at more steps. Step-MFT filters candidate strategies based on a successful trajectory and action difference from a no-strategy policy.

## 2. Findings supported by the assessed source
In the ALFWorld Qwen3-4B-Instruct ablation, dynamic memory plus synthesis achieved 65.5% success in the forced-refresh condition versus 59.3% without synthesis and 45.2% with neither. The paper reports relative gains up to 13% ALFWorld and 11% WebShop for training-free AdaMEM; compare only with its stated baselines. Refresh cadence creates an explicit accuracy-token trade-off.

The findings apply to the paper's studied population, benchmark and method. A derived design for Routin is an engineering hypothesis, not an observed effect of the paper.

## 3. Assumptions, caveats and counterexamples
The paper explicitly identifies strategy inertia when low-refresh self-evaluation mistakenly keeps a failing plan. It excludes failed trajectories from the long-term bank because agents may overgeneralize them. The fine-tuning selection rule uses a hypothetical baseline action and sparse final reward; action change is necessary for positive advantage under the paper's deterministic premises, not evidence of causal benefit in all settings. ALFWorld/WebShop are not social coordination or email safety.

## 4. Consequence for Routin's cognitive architecture
Routin should retrieve verified experience at material state transitions, synthesize an ephemeral strategy, and invalidate it through typed external evidence rather than model self-approval. Maintain failed episodes for diagnosis while not promoting them as general procedures. Separate procedure certification from within-task strategy adaptation.

## 5. Cross-paper mechanism and what it enables
Connect MAGE P046 to repair affected execution branches; ExpeL P051 to validated experience abstractions; Memory-R1 P055 to explicit write control; MemPABench P041 to behavioral policy checks. This suggests a three-gate loop: retrieve verified episodes -> synthesize current strategy -> validate action and observed effects.

## 6. Specific falsification and baseline
Ablate never-refresh, every-step refresh, model-decided refresh, and hard-evidence-triggered refresh on identical tasks and equal compute budgets. Measure invalid strategy persistence, downstream verified outcomes, critical permission violations and cost. Include a scenario where one failure creates a misleading negative rule.
