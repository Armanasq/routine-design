## 1. Research problem and actual methodology
MAGE uses an active hierarchical execution-state tree rather than retrieving isolated semantically similar notes. Bottom nodes record action-observation transitions; top nodes summarize completed subgoals. Grow records progress, Compress consolidates completed boundaries, Maintain validates and Revise restores the failed boundary, preserving unaffected progress and diagnostic branches.

## 2. Findings supported by the assessed source
On MemoryArena, reported task success improvements over comparison systems range 7.8–20.4 percentage points in the aggregate comparison and token consumption falls 55.1%. The ablation on shopping shows 39.33% task success for full MAGE vs 32.00% without Compress, 32.67% without Maintain and 35.33% without Revise. Paper also notes that generic RAG systems can use MORE tokens than long context on some frequent-update tasks due to extra retrieval/reasoning.

The findings apply to the paper's studied population, benchmark and method. A derived design for Routin is an engineering hypothesis, not an observed effect of the paper.

## 3. Assumptions, caveats and counterexamples
Its benchmark is a controlled synthetic/task environment, not live social negotiation. The Maintain step uses an LLM judge of summary validity; such a model may miss errors in unseen provider-side state. "Revise" changes the model's active branch; it cannot undo an irreversible email or externally accepted calendar invitation. The arXiv v1 details inspected may differ from later EMNLP camera-ready version.

## 4. Consequence for Routin's cognitive architecture
Routin should attach execution-state checkpoints to real-world responsibility transitions and distinguish *logical rollback of the plan* from *compensation/reconciliation of external effects*. Summaries must be validated against source-state snapshots and verifier evidence before being used as current truth.

## 5. Cross-paper mechanism and what it enables
Combine AdaMEM P045 for step-specific strategy generation with Garcia-Molina/Salem P027 for provider-side effect recovery, and Clark/Brennan P037 for mutual acceptance. A correct trajectory memory tree does not establish that a collaborator received or agreed to a message.

## 6. Specific falsification and baseline
Inject an error after a verified subgoal and an irreversible send. Compare full replanning, RAG retrieval and checkpoint-preserving revision; measure preserved valid subgoals, false completion, duplicate sends, token cost and correction time.
