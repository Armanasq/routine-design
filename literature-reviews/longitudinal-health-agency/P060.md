# P060 — A framework for longitudinal health AI agents

**Authors:** Georgianna Lin, Rencong Jiang, Noémie Elhadad and Xuhai Orson Xu  
**Publication:** 2026, Nature Health 1, 796–805 (Perspective)  
**Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s44360-026-00141-x  
**Review evidence:** `pubmed_full_text_pmc_article_selected_substantive_sections`, verified on 2026-11-16. This is an original critical synthesis; methods/results are inspected only to the degree indicated by the access label.

## 1. Question and investigated methods
Interdisciplinary perspective synthesized approximately 40 studies across personal health informatics, care continuity and HCI. Introduces four interacting dimensions: Coherence, Continuity, Adaptation and Agency. Illustrates with chronic, post-discharge and mental-health contexts.

## 2. Empirical result or theoretical contribution
Argues longitudinal health support needs active stewardship of evolving goals, partial outcomes, social roles and negotiated authority—not only memory retrieval. Emphasizes follow-up, partial/nonlinear progress, revisability, and user independence.

## 3. Boundaries, assumptions, negative evidence
This is a conceptual framework and hypothetical use cases, not a controlled trial demonstrating its value or an implemented safety-qualified system. It specifically cautions against dependence and hidden or overdirective behavior.

## 4. Concrete Routin policy or architectural implication
Routin should preserve unresolved health-related coordination threads while allowing the user to modify goals and assistance degree. It can organize clinician visits and questions, but must not become an autonomous treatment authority.

## 5. Cross-disciplinary mechanism synthesis
Transfer four-layer framing to all life domains only at the orchestration level; clinical semantics remain separate. Distinguish memory coherence from goal continuity and maintain consent as evolving and contestable.

## 6. Falsifiable test and acceptance
Track evolving goals in multiple simulated sessions; compare one-shot memory chatbot vs continuity-aware coordination, measuring missed follow-ups, user override, ungrounded clinical recommendations, goal revisions and dependence.
