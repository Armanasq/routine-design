## 1. Research problem and actual methodology
Systems characterization of ten representative agent-memory implementations, profiling construction, retrieval and serving costs across two benchmark suites and varying inference/backbone settings. It evaluates architectural cost, not personalized end-user wellbeing. Phase-aware energy and latency measurements reveal expensive construction in LLM-mediated memory systems.

## 2. Findings supported by the assessed source
In reported LongMemEval Qwen3-32B setting with 300 queries, BM25 achieved 47.0 accuracy in the authors' table, versus 42.7 for A-Mem and 32.0 for Mem0; the more complex systems consumed substantially more lifecycle construction energy. On a MemoryAgentBench macro-average, BM25 has high exact-recall accuracy, while structured approaches show category-specific strengths. The authors emphasize that no system is best across construction time, serving latency and accuracy.

The findings apply to the paper's studied population, benchmark and method. A derived design for Routin is an engineering hypothesis, not an observed effect of the paper.

## 3. Assumptions, caveats and counterexamples
Benchmark accuracy is dataset-specific and BM25's strong showing is partly driven by recall-heavy questions. Structured memory can still matter for mutation and multi-hop execution-state problems. Reported cost depends on hardware, chosen models, query arrivals and data distribution; don't extrapolate to Routin user cost. Stale asynchronous construction creates an additional operational trade-off.

## 4. Consequence for Routin's cognitive architecture
Routin should keep a cheap typed current-state projection on the critical path and treat expensive semantic/episodic consolidation as asynchronous. Profile construction+read+generation+maintenance+invalidations and measure cost per verified coordination outcome, not only answer accuracy.

## 5. Cross-paper mechanism and what it enables
Together with MemGPT P048 and MAGE P046, the results favor a bounded context compiler with indispensable current-state facts and optional retrieved histories rather than indiscriminate graph expansion. Together with Memory-R1 P055, write operations must have quality gates and explicit cost ceilings.

## 6. Specific falsification and baseline
Benchmark typed SQL + BM25, dense RAG, summarized memories and dynamic strategy retrieval at matched inference models. Compare correctness under time-varying source data, p95 latency, update freshness and total lifecycle cost. Reject systems whose added complexity fails a prespecified utility gate.
