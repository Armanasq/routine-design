# P074: Continual Learning Bench: Evaluating Frontier AI Systems in Real-World Stateful Environments

**Authors:** Parth Asawa, Christopher M. Glaze, Gabriel Orlanski, Ramya Ramakrishnan, Benji Xu, Asim Biswal, Vincent Sunn Chen, Frederic Sala, Matei Zaharia, Joseph E. Gonzalez  
**Year/venue:** 2026; arXiv:2606.05661 preprint  
**Canonical source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05661  
**Access level:** arxiv_abstract_and_introduction_html. The available abstracts and selected sections were inspected, but this is NOT a claim of exhaustive full-text analysis.

## What was studied and how
Expert-validated sequences in six domains have learnable latent structure: codebase familiarity, signal processing, outbreak/demand forecasting, database querying and strategic interaction. The benchmark explicitly measures improvement across sequential tasks rather than only absolute capability.

## What the evidence supports
Paper reports frequent overfitting to immediate observations and poor reuse across instances. In evaluated conditions, naive in-context learning outperformed systems designed for memory management. More memory components are not evidence of more learning.

## Boundaries and alternative explanations
Six expert-crafted domains still differ from real personal workflows; sequential improvement can be confounded by task order, backbone and context length. Abstract outcomes are not clinical or human productivity effects.

## Architectural inference for Routin (a hypothesis, not a study finding)
Maintain a reset-state/no-memory control and a later-task gain metric. Promote experience only if it improves future comparable but nonidentical tasks; track errors on unrelated tasks and memory construction cost.

## Consequential experiment and negative case
Use hidden latent-structure task sequences with tool names, providers and contexts changed across episodes. Compare reset, full-context, retrieved episodes and certified procedural learning; measure genuine incremental gain and negative transfer.

## What must be checked before a publication-grade claim
Inspect exact version, methods and results, sampling/selection, full tables/figures, statistics, comparison systems and counterexamples. Record stronger conflicting evidence if found. The research only informs the proposed controller; none of these papers independently demonstrates a universal, safe personal secretary or Routin's empirical benefit.
