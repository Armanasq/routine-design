## 1. Research problem and what was actually studied
Experiments associate strong focal goal pursuit with reduced accessibility of competing goals and examine moderators.

## 2. Established finding / intellectual contribution
Laboratory inhibition measures do not establish that software should suppress users' other obligations.

This statement is attributed to the cited research in its studied setting. It is **not** proof that the proposed Routin feature improves productivity.

## 3. Assumptions, limitations and alternative explanations
Protect user-declared goal capacity without hiding consequential commitments or forcing a goal the user no longer values.
