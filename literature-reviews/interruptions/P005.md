## 1. Research problem and what was actually studied
Four-study research program supports brief ready-to-resume planning as a way to reduce attention residue under anticipated resumption pressure.

## 2. Established finding / intellectual contribution
Does not prove that automatically generated summaries restore attention or that every task switch needs a resumption ritual.

This statement is attributed to the cited research in its studied setting. It is **not** proof that the proposed Routin feature improves productivity.
