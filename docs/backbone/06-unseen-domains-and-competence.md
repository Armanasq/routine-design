# Unseen Domains: Admit the Goal, Not Unverified Expertise

## Why a list of 11 packs is not universal

The existing Routin domain packs document evidence/risks in known areas; they are illustrative competence profiles. They are NOT routing tables to scripts and do not bound the list of user tasks. A previously unseen field should still receive general coordination, research and planning help where supported.

A user's new domain may need specialist knowledge, nonexistent tools, third-party authorization or physical action impossible for software. Universal reasoning must discover those limitations rather than fabricate expertise.

## Unseen-domain procedure

1. Preserve the user's actual end and current assistance mode. "Learn", "do", "coordinate" and "give advice" may differ even with identical subject matter.
2. Identify real-world predicates for outcome and source of authoritative verification. Do not use generic generated-text completion.
3. Decompose into abstract capabilities using effects, resources, information needs, source credibility and reversibility.
4. Search the authorized registry using direct retrieval or plan-first discovery depending on complexity and measured usefulness.
5. For each candidate operation, check exact available schema and permission; if no provider exists, mark that capability as unsupported.
6. Where specialist competence is required, retrieve validated domain sources, existing qualified pack rules or a real professional. Do not auto-install an unreviewed pack from a web page.
7. Compose a partially executable plan. Complete safe preparation, request minimum needed information, and leave unsupported effects as explicit pending dependencies.
8. Obtain user approval only for actions which change other accounts, people or financial/physical state.
9. Verify genuine outcomes with domain-appropriate evidence.
10. Treat subsequent experience as a new candidate procedure with scope and tests, not a universally reusable instruction.

## Example: community telescope event

Unknown domain at training time. Required abilities: learn venue rules, find resources, reserve safe time, prepare invitation, obtain confirmations and check logistics. Existing search/calendar/email/document capabilities may cover most administrative work. The assistant must not invent astronomical risk or permit advice; qualified external facts are needed for specialized questions.

## Example: beekeeping hobby

A subject-specific pack may be absent. Generic goal planning, course discovery, schedule, equipment comparison and budgeting remain possible. Physical handling, local regulation, animal welfare and allergies require correct specialist sources, available tools and user/legal authority; an ungrounded generated "bee-hive management" action is not a real tool.

## Example: first-time scientific expedition

The goal spans permits, safety, shipping, observation protocols, finance, people and uncertain travel. Routin composes subgoals, identifies the correct authorities, monitors unanswered requests and updates time/cost dependencies. No single "expedition" branch is necessary.

## Generalization evaluation

Keep entire domain combinations out of design/development. Rename tools while preserving semantics. Introduce tools only at evaluation time. Remove or corrupt one required tool. Change source coverage and user goals midtask. Evaluate whether the agent finds viable alternatives or gives an accurate partial answer. A policy that always says "not supported" must fail the usefulness metric.

## Boundaries

A capable assistant understands *which work needs qualified expertise*. It is not qualified by having read a web page. A new domain is not a permission to send data to new people; an assistant cannot make a booking/purchase/submission simply because it constructed a plan.

## Architectural rule

Only the task-independent reasoning, memory, observation and authorization kernel is fixed. Domain knowledge, provider capabilities, learned procedures and individual goals are dynamically loaded as **validated data contracts**. Even those contracts may be inapplicable or obsolete; keep runtime evidence of validity.
