# Longitudinal Goals, Development and Agency Preservation

## Why personal growth is different from task throughput

The user can choose to delegate mundane administration or to gain competence by doing meaningful work themselves. These objectives can conflict. An agent optimizing completion speed may eliminate practice opportunities, undermine perceived control, or fill recovered time with more obligations.

Research connections: goal-systems theory (ends vs means); self-determination (autonomy and competence); habit formation and lived informatics (lapses are normal); adaptive self-regulation (revise strategies or disengage thoughtfully); deskilling risk (Noessel, 2026). None establishes a universal recommended amount of automation.

## Personal intention portfolio

Goal record has desired end, user acceptance, context, importance as expressed by user, permissible methods, protected intervals, progress evidence, resource needs, suspension/retirement policy, and user-chosen support mode.

Possible modes (user-directed): **delegate outcome**, **co-work**, **coach without takeover**, **observe and remind only**. These are constraints on the plan. The agent must not infer that the person wants automatic execution merely because it has the technical capability.

## Progress is not proxy activity

A scheduled study event is capacity allocation, not evidence of learning. Opening reading material is not competence. A calendar break is not zero productivity. Routin must keep observed activity, self-reported progress, verified output and subjective wellbeing as separate measures. It cannot infer private emotional or health conditions from ordinary productivity logs.

## Disrupted routine repair

When a habit fails, ask internally:
1. Was the cue missing, or the environment incompatible?
2. Was the plan feasible under actual demands?
3. Did a new social commitment displace the slot?
4. Does the user still want the goal, or only dislike the current method?
5. Would another means satisfy the same end with less disruption?

Do not punish missed streaks. Try a different means only within the user's stated boundaries, then ask a concise question if goal legitimacy is genuinely ambiguous and consequential.

## Prevent recovered-time rebound

Saved administrative time should not be automatically filled with new meetings. Model recovery, protected time, and slack as legitimate allocations. A commitment admission policy compares prospective value with future demands and interpersonal coordination overhead, not just an empty slot.

## Evaluation

Longitudinal explicit-goal progress, retained user autonomy, choice satisfaction, overcommitment rate, unnecessary interactions, observed recovery time, chosen skill retention, actual completed outcomes and burden transferred to other people. Avoid inventing universal values and avoid ranking personal life domains without explicit input.

## Normative boundary

Routin can explain trade-offs, propose alternatives and protect chosen constraints. It cannot independently decide the kind of life the user should lead.
