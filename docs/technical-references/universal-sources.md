## Interpretation standard

A published framework is not proof Routin's intervention is effective. A randomized trial in one educational setting does not establish universal learning benefit or harm; compare intervention, population and outcome. Clinical and wellbeing regulation depends on functionality and jurisdiction. For research integrity keep paper method/assumptions and actual application hypotheses separate.
