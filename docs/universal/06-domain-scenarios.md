# Representative Universal Scenarios: From Intent to Verified Outcome

Synthetic scenarios are not claims about user behavior. Each exercises different semantics while using the same base responsibility/evidence state.

## Learning (course, coding, language, research)
**User:** "Help me learn system identification for an exam. I only have short blocks this week."
- End: independent mastery and retention; support mode: teach/practice.
- Domain: learning goals, current knowledge and evaluation opportunities.
- Coordinator: budget study intervals, identify prerequisites, choose practice questions/retrieval checks when appropriate, reschedule under workload conflicts.
- Evidence: material accessed != understood. Independent responses and spaced follow-ups provide stronger evidence of learning than study minutes.
- Safety: avoid always giving full solutions where practice is the objective; do not force tutoring if user requests a quick answer.
- Outcome: measurable independent performance at an agreed interval; partial progress remains open.

## Fitness and gym
**User:** "Help me maintain three gym sessions, but I have a new shoulder issue."
- End: user-chosen consistent activity; not a medical diagnosis.
- Pack: training schedule and generic educational guidance, source quality and explicit restrictions.
- Coordinator: preserve rest/recovery capacity, seek safe alternatives, avoid schedule collapse during a busy week.
- Boundary: no automatic high-load progression or diagnosis; concerning symptoms warrant professional input.
- Outcome: completed chosen activities and optional user-valued performance measures; not inferred health improvement from exercise minutes.

## Wellbeing and recovery
**User:** "I'm overloaded. Stop filling my evening with errands."
- End: protected recovery time and reduced unnecessary intervention.
- Coordinator: defer or negotiate incoming commitments, batch low-risk items and preserve explicitly reserved free time.
- Learning: repeated late work is workload evidence, not a preference for more late work.
- Outcome: protected interval respected, lower correction burden, user-reported meaningful effect if voluntarily assessed.
- Safety: no inferred psychological diagnosis.

## Health coordination
**User:** "Organize my lab results and help me prepare for my appointment."
- End: accurate record organization and an informed clinician discussion.
- Pack: health-data provenance, units/time/reference ranges when provided, professional-boundary controls.
- Coordinator: organize authorized documents, appointments and clinician questions; remind and follow up if requested.
- Boundary: do not independently diagnose, prescribe, or claim clinical urgency classification on insufficient evidence; appropriately flag limitations and escalation.
- Outcome: organized factual summary and appointment coordination; treatment decisions remain with qualified clinician.

## Work project and task management
**User:** "Make sure we deliver the proposal Friday, even if the reviewer is late."
- End: confirmed deliverable; dependencies and counterparty handoffs.
- Coordinator: maintain expected review, time-budget preparation, propose scope renegotiation on overload, preserve actual send/receipt/acceptance distinctions.
- Outcome: deliverable verified, with unresolved recipient agreement separately tracked.

## Applications (academic, jobs, grants, visas, programs)
**User:** "Apply to two funded programs; help with requirements, references and deadlines."
- End: valid authorized submissions, not form autofill count.
- Domain: program eligibility from official current source, required documents and privacy.
- Coordinator: requirements/dependency graph, letters requested and confirmed, preparation time, eligibility uncertainty, versioned drafts, explicit submission approval.
- Outcome: authoritative submission confirmation; interview/offer decisions remain separate states. No invented funding or guaranteed acceptance.

## Communication and relationships
**User:** "Follow up with the collaborator if they haven't replied."
- End: mutually understood next step, not a sent email.
- Coordinator: require current source coverage and expected reply window; draft respectfully with relationship-specific bounds; send only with current grant.
- Outcome: sent, responded, agreed, fulfilled tracked independently; no follow-up if source sync stale.

## Family and life administration
**User:** "Coordinate school pickup and protect Friday family time."
- End: accurate arrangement involving multiple people and constraints.
- Coordinator: verify involved parties, tentative vs confirmed agreements, location/time-sensitive conditions, authorized disclosure boundaries.
- Outcome: agreement with responsible adult verified; mere calendar event creation insufficient.

## Travel and logistics
**User:** "Prepare a trip within my stated budget and avoid morning flights."
- End: feasible approved travel plan.
- Coordinator: compare cost/constraints using fresh offers and conditions; prepare booking options; request explicit payment and reservation approval.
- Outcome: provider booking confirmation and current reservation terms; speculative search result never a purchased ticket.

## Personal finance and paperwork
**User:** "Remind me about bills and help prepare the claim."
- End: accurate document handling and lawful user-approved filing/payment.
- Coordinator: deadlines, document provenance, recurring bills and expiration terms; prepare but do not transfer money without strong grants.
- Outcome: filed claim/confirmed payment separately from prepared form; no financial advice or predictions presented as guaranteed.

## Universal lesson

These scenarios share objects—intent, resources, parties, evidence, commitments, capabilities, constraints, intervention, verified outcome. The **domain contract** determines how a claim is validated, what tools may be used, what requires specialist review, and what counts as success. Universality is a compositional property, not a claim of unlimited expertise.
