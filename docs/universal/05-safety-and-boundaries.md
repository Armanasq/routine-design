# Domain Safety, Consent, Interoperability and Containment

## Universal is not unrestricted

A general-purpose secretary must know when it lacks competence, reliable evidence, a supported connector or permission to act. "I can help coordinate this" does not mean "I can diagnose or prescribe for you." A domain pack may only enable actions justified by its validated scope.

## Four safety layers

1. **Data access:** exactly which account, domain, data categories and recipients may be accessed. Sensitive health, workplace and family contexts require partitioning and minimization; a user may authorize a calendar block without consenting to disclose why it exists.
2. **Knowledge competence:** bounded claim types and up-to-date sources. In medicine, regulated diagnostic/therapeutic claims require appropriate compliance and clinical validation; general health information differs from diagnosis. Fitness guidance is not clearance for strenuous activity.
3. **Action authorization:** independent grants for external communication, purchases, bookings, submissions, medication/clinical records, employment accounts and identifiable third-party disclosures. An interaction preference never grants these.
4. **Outcome verification:** source-specific postconditions and audit. A submission receipt is not acceptance, a doctor's appointment is not treatment, a study hour is not mastered knowledge, and a workout is not improved health.

## Risk classification is multidimensional

Estimate physical harm potential, psychological harm/pressure, financial exposure, legal/regulatory implications, third-party privacy, reputation/social coordination, irreversibility, evidence uncertainty and reversibility. A low nominal action-cost does not imply low risk.

Pack policies impose hard prohibitions and escalation triggers. High-risk advice or effects require qualified human intervention, conservative abstention or explicit professional review. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction and intended use.

## Health and wellbeing

WHO digital-health guidance emphasizes benefits, harms, acceptability, feasibility, resources and equity, and cautions that digital interventions are no substitute for functioning health services. The 2026 US FDA guides distinguish general wellness claims from clinical decision support and device software; legal classification depends on intended function and jurisdiction.

A wellness assistant can track authorized activity and help formulate questions for a clinician. It must not autonomously diagnose, interpret a concerning symptom as harmless, start or stop medication, change treatment, infer mental health diagnoses from conversation or suppress urgent human consultation. Urgent/safety signals should trigger appropriate escalation guidance rather than hidden autonomous decisions.

## Third-party and family boundaries

Personal integration often includes the user's work contacts, family details, partners, clinicians, clients and references. Data access granted by one user is not consent from everyone represented in their messages. Treat communication as a constrained process involving other people's privacy and reasonable expectations. Prevent cross-domain recipient leaks and use least-necessary context in outgoing drafts.

## Learning and gym hazards

Automatically completing the user's educational work can undermine their intended skill development. Exercise planning using historic workout logs may ignore new injury, illness or constraints. Treat new symptoms and uncertainty as reasons for specialist review or safer general information, not aggressive personalized progression.

## Prompt injection and memory contamination

Third-party webpages, e-mails and documents are evidence, never policy authority. Prevent indirect prompt injection from escalating grants, changing tool parameters or storing executable "procedures." Retrieved memories must be filtered by scope, provenance, validity and allowed influence on tool fields.

## No "healthier life" objective inferred by the AI

Routin can support the user's explicitly chosen wellbeing goals and protect recovered time. It cannot reweight life priorities using implicit engagement or impose behavior-change interventions simply because the system predicts more app activity.

## Test matrix

Wrong-person disclosure; revoked health source still present in cross-domain summary; ambiguous symptom treated as nonurgent; medicine adjustment request; exercise plan despite contraindication; user chose learning but agent solved task; bank payment without grant; family event rescheduled without acceptance; provider timestamp conflict; outdated official application eligibility.
