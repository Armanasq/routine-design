# Whole-Life Planning Without a Universal Value Function

## Problem

A person cannot allocate the same hour to a professional deadline, a learning session, exercise and family obligations. The domains cannot be reduced to one universal priority score, and the assistant must not decide that professional productivity is more important than health, rest or relationships.

## Objective decomposition

At decision epoch t the state includes: source evidence/health, currently accepted commitments, candidate requests, explicit short and long-horizon goals, protected capacity, estimated durations, role/relationship obligations, personal decision mode, domain safety policies, current permissions and uncertainty.

First enforce:
1. Current authority, source and identity constraints.
2. Accepted/confirmed external commitments and their legitimate change protocols.
3. User-declared hard boundaries and chosen protected goals.
4. Domain-specific safety/evidence requirements.
5. Scheduling/time capacity feasibility and risk of critical omission.

Only among feasible, permitted alternatives compare the vector: missed obligation risk, user effort, counterparty effort, goal displacement, schedule churn, recovery/rest impacts, financial cost, uncertainty and model cost. If choices reflect genuinely unresolved user values, ask one consequential question instead of inventing weights.

## Commitment admission across life domains

An incoming meeting proposal competes with work, learning, physical training, recovery and family activity. It is not already accepted. Evaluate alternatives **before** admission. With probabilistic durations, include contingency and preparation; unobserved fatigue or emotional state must not be guessed. Protected personal intervals are legitimate constraints even if no calendar title reveals their purpose.

A late task need not automatically override a high-value goal. Sometimes negotiate scope/date rather than move the user's routine. If a hard accepted obligation conflicts with a user-chosen activity, present trade-offs rather than silently modifying either.

## Counterfactual planning and interventions

Compare at least:
- status quo, monitoring silently;
- low-effort preparation without external effects;
- user-permitted internal reschedule;
- request clarification or approval;
- propose alternative to counterparty with grant;
- direct permitted action with verifiable postcondition.

Evaluate **last safe intervention time** and current interruptibility separately from importance. When domain safety conditions require prompt human attention, the ordinary user-attention cost must not suppress critical action.

## Domain outcome incommensurability

Do not calculate "learning points + gym points + email points" and optimize their sum. Use constraint satisfaction, Pareto comparisons, explicitly chosen preferences and user negotiation when alternatives are not comparable.

A person's study mastery may be delayed and require assessment. Fitness adaptation is not guaranteed by attendance. An application can remain undecided months after a valid submission. All are meaningful *partial trajectories* and should not become false binary task completion.

## Reasoning under changing context

Plan stability and user corrections are costs. Recompute only dependent subgraphs when a source/goal/capacity change materially affects feasibility. Avoid repeated full rescheduling and preserve verified external agreements. If a connected source is stale, negative inferences are unknown and cannot trigger unsanctioned outreach.

## Example

User says: "I have my exam next month, keep gym three times a week, apply to two programs, and don't fill my evenings."

The coordinator should create separate goals (retained mastery; consistent chosen activity; valid application submissions; protected evenings), identify fixed dates and official eligibility uncertainties, budget meaningful study practice, prepare application document/dependency workflows, plan feasible optional workouts and reject automatic evening commitments. If the total portfolio is infeasible, propose **specific trade-offs** instead of hiding overload or fabricating an optimal personal value judgment.

## Measurement

Compare protected-time integrity, missed verified deadlines, probability calibration, actual learning performance (when consented and measured), missed personal goals, number of unnecessary clarifications, user correction time and overcommitment rate. Report categories separately. Cross-domain improvements can be harmful if a favored domain improves by displacing another.
