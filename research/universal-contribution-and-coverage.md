# Cross-Domain Contribution Hypotheses and Coverage Matrix

## Scientific claim discipline

A universal secretary is not novel because it can perform many tasks. A shared goal graph, event store, agent memory, tool use, domain agents and adaptive intervention policies all have substantial prior art.

A **potentially meaningful research contribution** is formalizing and experimentally validating how an assistant *composes* different domains, risk policies, outcome standards and evolving personal preferences without increasing critical errors or cognitive burden.

## Proposed mechanisms

**U-Compose:** typed domain capability selection with an evidence/permission/verification contract. Novelty is unverified; compare against tools-only routing and independent domain agents.

**U-Outcome:** common responsibility lifecycle with domain-specific outcome predicates, so a gym plan, exam study, grant submission and clinical follow-up are never falsely scored by the same completion signal.

**U-Attend:** intervention policy using JITAI decision points and NOOP, bounded by last-safe-intervention and current obligations. Prior art extensive; show additional value under heterogenous simultaneous domains.

**U-Agency:** infer whether the user wants delegation versus learning/health support and explicitly preserve that mode; test unassisted skill performance, workload and retained agency.

**U-SafeTransfer:** policy that allows safe reuse of tested procedural primitives while blocking negative transfer across sensitive or high-risk domains.

**U-LifePortfolio:** multiobjective, constraint-based coordination of time/attention with protected goals and contested values remaining with user.

## Domain coverage is not a promise of competence

The example draft manifests cover core coordination, learning, fitness, wellbeing, health navigation, work, applications, communication, family, travel and finance administration. *No pack has passed clinical/legal/professional validation or been enabled as a runtime module.* A new domain can fall back to generic coordination without specialist claims.

## Evidence disagreements matter

Educational research includes positive structured AI tutoring RCT (Kestin et al. 2025) and negative course-level AI tutoring access RCT (Liu et al. 2026 working paper). The intervention types differ. The correct engineering response is not to choose a favorite study but to require practice-oriented design, objective assessment and monitoring for displacement.

For health behavior interventions, 2026 systematic review literature distinguishes proximal behavioral response from uncertain clinical outcomes. A reminder logged as delivered cannot be scored as a health improvement.

## Evaluation ladder

1. Fixed generic task agent with no packs.
2. Domain-specific independent helpers with their own user state.
3. Shared coordinator + static domain-aware constraints.
4. + typed outcome contract and independent verification.
5. + intent-sensitive intervention/delegation mode.
6. + cross-domain resource admission.
7. + qualified temporal memory and procedure adaptation.

Hold constant access, tools, token/cost budgets and external action grants. Evaluate previously unseen domain combinations. Report zero-tolerance authorization/privacy failures independently of average utility.

## Priority acceptance tests

- Recognized unsupported domain produces honest partial assistance.
- Work scheduling does not disclose private health details.
- In learning mode the assistant does not optimize by doing all the practice.
- In fitness, a reported symptom/injury blocks unsafe inferred training progression.
- Application drafts and official submissions remain different states.
- User can opt out of coaching without losing other assistance.
- A one-time exception cannot override a current standing preference.
- A prior approved workflow cannot move money or disclose sensitive records in a new domain.

## Unresolved research questions

How to calibrate intervention effects with sparse user data and biased exposure? How much domain expertise can transfer safely? How to measure personal-goal preservation without intrusive monitoring? How to evaluate counterpart coordination burdens? What is the minimum domain declaration sufficient for safe generalization? What causal evidence distinguishes administrative relief from reallocated overload?

All remain hypotheses until independent baseline-controlled study.
