# Falsifiable research hypotheses

H1 **Commitment admission:** including admission negotiation reduces accepted infeasible obligations vs calendar-gap scheduling, without increasing unjustified declines.
H2 **Attention orchestration:** joint subject/timing/detail/actor policy reduces net administrative effort vs static ranking, subject to no regression in critical omissions.
H3 **Temporal beliefs:** scoped belief revision reduces stale-preference errors vs flat RAG memory.
H4 **Goal protection:** planning around explicit goals preserves long-term goal progress under disruptions better than unconstrained reoptimization.
H5 **Outcome verification:** remote postcondition checks reduce false completion vs tool-response success.
H6 **Learning bias:** exposure-aware feedback produces lower preference drift/goal bias than naive click-based learning.
H7 **Resumption:** optional ready-to-resume capsules reduce resumption errors and delay under meaningful interruptions.
H8 **Selective questions:** value-of-information policy reduces unnecessary clarifications without worsening high-impact decisions.
H9 **Time uncertainty:** probabilistic duration forecasts reduce infeasible plans and schedule churn vs point durations.
H10 **Agency:** deliberate-practice protected delegation preserves chosen skill development compared with maximal automation.

Each hypothesis requires predeclared populations, counterexamples, hard guardrail thresholds, time windows and analysis plan. See [evaluation protocol](evaluation.md).
