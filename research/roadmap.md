# Research and implementation gates

**Phase 0 — Research corpus:** verified catalog, per-paper reviews, licensing, mechanism synthesis and knowledge gaps. Gate: source provenance and evidence quality documented.

**Phase 1 — Deterministic coordination kernel:** event ledger, typed claims, responsibilities, permissions, waiting and outcome states, test fixtures. Gate: replay and adversarial state reconciliation.

**Phase 2 — Temporal personal model:** explicit preferences, bitemporal updates, supersession, privacy/deletion, current-validity retrieval. Gate: reduced stale memory errors.

**Phase 3 — Time/admission:** duration estimates, fixed/protected constraints, capacity forecasts and stable alternative schedules. Gate: calibrated feasible planning.

**Phase 4 — Attention/clarification:** interruption policy, value of question, level-of-detail selection and audit decisions. Gate: controlled comparison and critical omission protection.

**Phase 5 — Effectful execution:** grant enforcement, durable workflows, unknown outcomes, read-after-write, human approval. Gate: no unauthorized effects and low false completion.

**Phase 6 — Learning and personal growth:** drift adaptation, exposure-corrected feedback, self-impact, optional skill-preserving delegation. Gate: longitudinal benefit, correctability and rollback.

**Phase 7 — Product implementation:** exceptionally minimal user surface after backend output contract stabilizes; validate usability and accessibility before aesthetic optimization.

Every gate is an acceptance criterion, not a calendar estimate. No production implementation is claimed by this repository.
