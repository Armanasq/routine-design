**P0 formalize ontology:** schema for actor identity, modal speech acts, temporal obligations, waiting states, outcome evidence, permission grants, and negative-inference freshness.

**P1 attention study:** immediate, breakpoint, digest and silent preparation; resumption; last-safe-intervention calculation; omission constraints.

**P1 execution test:** idempotency, timeout-unknown, provider-side verification, state reconciliation and irreversible operations.

**P2 longitudinal pilot:** net administrative minutes, actual deadline completion, skill preservation, self-selected goal progress, correction burden and unnecessary interventions.

**P2 competitor protocols:** fixed user scenario transcripts with permissioned demo accounts where permitted; independent testing of Orb, Kai, Town, Sam; document verified abilities and unresolved gaps rather than asserting unsupported shortcomings.
