## Deficiencies identified

1. **Citation proliferation:** many entries map a paper to a feature without showing what logical assumption permits the transfer. Remedy: formal proof obligations and mechanism-specific falsification.
2. **Abstract-to-architecture leap:** the earlier reviews sometimes imply that a mechanism will benefit Routin because a source studied a related task. Remedy: separate empirical source result from proposed implementation and null/counterexample.
3. **Weak social epistemology:** sending was distinguished from completion, but shared understanding/coordination effort was underspecified. Remedy: grounding and articulation models.
4. **Intent semantics:** desires, tentative plans, accepted social commitments and authorization could be conflated. Remedy: typed intention/commitment model and approval states.
5. **Memory realization:** temporal retrieval was specified, but applying an interaction preference and actually behaving accordingly were not separate acceptance gates. Remedy: MemPABench-derived selection and realization tests.
6. **Counterfactual feedback:** implicit clicks reflect the policy's own exposure; naive learning can reinforce its own earlier choices. Remedy: exposure logging, explicit-confirmation precedence, no unknown counterfactual claims.
7. **Truth maintenance:** a memory system needs contradiction, contraction and scope logic rather than more stored text; AGM offers a normative lens, not turnkey code.
8. **No executable decision core:** specification was previously prose-only. Remedy: a deterministic pure reference kernel and adversarial tests. Reference code intentionally does not pretend to be production or to calibrate a user's priorities.
